💡 Quick First-Aid & Triage Advice for Seniors

❤️ Chest Pain / Distress

Sit upright comfortably. Loosen tight clothing. Stay calm while assistance is en route.

🩸 Bleeding / Wounds

Apply firm, direct pressure on the wound with a clean cloth. Elevate the area slightly.

🧠 Sudden Dizziness / Weakness

Sit down immediately to prevent falls. Sip small amounts of water slowly.
